The two teams that had not won a game all season posted the two best scores of Week 5, and neither of their games was close.

Big Dick of the Week: David, Drain the Swamp, 174.48. A week after the lowest score of his career, the two-time defending champion has his first win.

Little Bitch of the Week: Erick, King In The North, 105.00. Every one of his starters was done by Sunday night, and he waited through all of Monday to learn it wasn't enough. He edged out the commissioner for this award by four hundredths of a point.

Drew, the league's other winless team, finished a hair behind David for the week's top score. The commissioner's unbeaten season ended in the Game of the Week against Chris, whose quarterback carried a backfield that did almost nothing. And Dylan caught Erick late on Monday night with the last man he had playing.

Bonus: Massive Cuck, for losing by the most, goes to Hoa. $9. It is the one bonus in this league you collect from the wrong end of the scoreboard.
