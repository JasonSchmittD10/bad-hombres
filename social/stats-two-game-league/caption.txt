The tightest Week 6 before this was 2024, at three games. Before this year, first and last had never been within two games after Week 3.

The 4-2 teams should feel good: 11 of 16 have made the playoffs. The 2-4 teams should remember that the 2024 title game was David vs. Hoa, and both of them were 2-4 at this point.
