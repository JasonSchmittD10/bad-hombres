Week 2 · The Preview

Four and Four, Zack

Erick and Zack lead a slate rated in Money Shots, the commissioner is in first place and nervous about it, and Pastor Wes has spoken.

From the BoothThe Bad Hombres beat · Week 2 · Sep 17, 2026

Before anyone else gets roasted, a word about the commissioner. Jason Schmitt is in first place after one week, which would mean something if he hadn't finished on the podium in this league five times without winning it once. Hold the applause until roughly never.

Now, Zack. Zack did everything right last week, scored like a grown man, and lost anyway — to Adam, who runs a fantasy team the way a golden retriever runs through a sprinkler: total commitment, zero understanding. Zack is 0-1. Adam is 1-0. You and I both know which of those two men has been insufferable about it.

A week like that should humble a person. A walk. A deep breath. Maybe a vegetable. Instead the schedule hands Zack the one manager in this league he is exactly, perfectly, maddeningly even with: Erick. They have played eight times. FOUR AND FOUR, ZACK. THE UNIVERSE LOOKED AT YOU TWO AND DECLINED TO GET INVOLVED.

Anyway. The goddamn games.

The Games

All six, rated for sheer watchability on a scale of one to five Money Shots.

FIVE MONEY SHOTS

Erick vs. Zack. The Game of the Week. Erick put up the second-best score in the league last week and won. Zack put up the fourth-best and got robbed in broad daylight. Yahoo likes Zack by a little this time, and Yahoo also liked Zack last week, so let's all agree Yahoo is the guy at the end of the bar who is very sure about the weather. (For what it's worth, I hope Zack gets this one. Nobody in this league has earned a boring Sunday more.)

FOUR MONEY SHOTS

Jason vs. Wes. Church against state. Jason has won five of their seven meetings, which he calls a rivalry and everyone else calls a pattern. Wes is 1-0 and serene. Jason is 1-0 and checking the app at dinner, which is a different thing.

Tola vs. Matt. Matt won last week's $9 because Josh Allen had a nice afternoon, which means Matt has now been paid by a man who does not know his name. Tola outscored most of the league last week, lost anyway, and has gone quiet in a way that should concern all of us. Tola owns the series. Matt owns the $9.

THREE MONEY SHOTS

Hoa vs. Adam. Pastor Wes has made this his Lock, which means a small committee in heaven has been convened on a Thursday. These two have played eight times and split them right down the middle, so the Lord is working with no information whatsoever. It has never stopped Him before. Adam, meanwhile, is coming off the best week of his adult life and has told each of us about it personally.

David vs. Dylan. Two 0-1 teams: one belongs to the two-time defending champion, and one belongs to Dylan. Yahoo, for reasons it has not shared, has Dylan favored. Let us pause for a moment to salute that kind of courage. The series is 3-3, which is the nicest thing a spreadsheet has ever said about Dylan.

TWO MONEY SHOTS

None.

ONE MONEY SHOT

Drew vs. Chris. This site's preseason No. 1 against a team named for a Star Wars pun its quarterback has not yet honored. Drew has won four of their five meetings. The one reason to tune in: it's Raw-Dogged week, the biggest blowout takes the $9, and this is the biggest projected margin on the board. Chris. Buddy. Be the blowout, not the brochure for one.

Pastor Wes's Lock of the Week

Hoa over Adam. Wes is 0-0.

Lineups lock at kickoff. Set them.
