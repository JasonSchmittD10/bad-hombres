Week 8 · The Preview

Erick and Wes Both Want the Top Bunk

Two 4-3 teams meet right on the playoff line, and only one of them sleeps up there Sunday night.

From the BoothMike Hunt · Week 8 · Oct 29, 2026

Four men are 4-3 this morning, and two of them have to play each other.

I'll be fair about the room. Chris and Adam are 5-2 and can see the playoffs from the couch. Tola, Matt, Zack and Hoa are a game back with their faces pressed to the glass. Six seats, and the four guys in the middle are treating the last of them like assigned seating.

But.

Erick and Wes are the only two of those four who meet on Sunday. Everybody else draws a stranger. These two draw a mirror. Same record, same side of the line, and Monday night one of them is 4-4, looking up at the man who stood next to him on Thursday.

Who blinks? The guy who lost three straight and then remembered who he was? Or the pastor, who has spent two months telling this league what to do and now has to go DO it?

That's a playoff game wearing an October jersey. Treat it like one.

The Games

Game of the Week: Erick vs. Wes. Erick leads the series 4-3, about as close as two grown men can stand without somebody asking for room. Yahoo likes the pastor this week, and the pastor has learned not to trust anybody who likes him on a Thursday. Make your pick before kickoff.

Chris vs. Zack. Zack has spent seven weeks drawing opponents on the best Sunday of their lives. This week the schedule just hands him the best team in the league. Zack, I'm sorry.

Jason vs. Hoa. The commissioner was 4-0 and has lost three straight without ever announcing a rebuild. Hoa just had the best week in the league. Jason leads the series, which is the kind of thing a man brings up when he hasn't won in three weeks.

David vs. Adam. The two-time defending champion is the Beer Mile favorite, and he draws a man tied for first. They're 3-3 all-time, the only place David is even with anybody right now. The title is gone. The dignity is still on the table.

Tola vs. Dylan. Dylan has been 4-3 at this exact point twice before and walked it down to 6-8 both times, so he knows this hallway and where it lets out. Tola scores like a contender and gets scored on like a piñata.

Drew vs. Matt. Drew's lineup got a public talking-to on Tuesday; Sunday we find out whether he read it. Matt is a game out of a seat himself and in no mood to host a comeback. Lose this, and Drew's best-team-in-the-league argument becomes something people say at his memorial.

Pastor Wes's Lock of the Week

Brothers, turn with me to the Book of Second Carpools, chapter nine. "And there was but one seat left in the van, and the brethren looked upon it, and no man spoke."

Youth retreat. Fifteen-passenger van, sixteen passengers, one seat left by the only vent that blew cold. It was my van. I paid for the gas on my own card, and the church still owes me for that, the receipt is in the glovebox if the finance committee is reading. But a shepherd does not take the last seat, brothers. A shepherd rides on the cooler. Four hours on a Coleman full of deviled eggs and Capri Suns, every pothole on I-35, and I have never felt closer to the Lord or to the pavement.

So I have looked upon this week, and I have seen one seat, and two men reaching for it, and one of them is me. Erick over Wes. Amen.

(Wes: 3–3.)

Lineups lock at kickoff. Set them.
