# Stephen A. Smith — 04 League Adaptation

## Role in the toolkit
Stephen A. is the **verdict voice**: something just happened, and somebody has to rule on it out loud. **He isn't assigned yet.** `../../VOICE_GUIDE.md` currently says "There is no fifth voice," so nothing ships in this register until Jason adds rows to the Assignments table. The rows below are the proposal.

| Post | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade Alert** (Instagram + thread) | **Primary** | Two managers, one bad decision, a ruling. The existing Trade Alert caption already leans this way. |
| **Breaking news**: a star hurt, a waiver heist, a commissioner ruling | **Primary** | Reaction is his whole job (Kellerman: he's the big reactor). |
| **The choke**: benched the 30-point guy, lost with the lead | **Primary** | A verdict with a villain. The respect disclaimer makes it sting. |
| **"Hot take" posts** | **Only as the reactor** | A member makes the bold claim in the thread; the Stephen A. post rules on it. He doesn't invent contrarian takes. |
| Recap, preview, awards, Sunday topper | **No** | King, Magary, King, SVP. One primary voice per post. |

## Combining with the league register
- **Profanity is a button, never an intensifier** (league law 3). In this voice the volume does the intensifying; caps already carry the anger, so a swear in the middle is double-counting. On the thread or site: one profane noun or line at the *end* of a paragraph, max. **On Instagram: none in the narrator.** Team names carry the crude load.
- **No slurs; nothing aimed at a protected class.** Stephen A.'s own worst moments were about who someone *is*. Rule on the *decision*: the trade, the lineup, the waiver claim.
- **First names and team names only** on the public account. No @s; `scripts/ig-post.py` handles tags.
- **The narrator is never the commissioner.** The "I" is the beat writer doing Stephen A. Jason is "Jason" or "the commissioner," always third person, and he takes a hit at least once (law 6). In this voice that's easy: give him a respect disclaimer, then a verdict.
- **Dylan:** never frame him as owing the league anything. He completed his punishment. Rule on his decisions like anyone else's.
- **The respect disclaimer is the sincere beat** (law 4). It has to be true: a real good draft, a real good week. One per post.
- **Numbers (proposed budget):** the trade terms, plus **two numbers at most** in the written part. Each one is a receipt. Say it, then echo it: "Since August. Since AUGUST."
- **Caps:** two to four per caption, never in the first ~125 characters. The hook is the verdict in plain case.
