# Used this season — check before writing, append after publishing

Every recap, preview, Monday Night Watch and Hard Numbers post reads this first. Nothing
here comes back unless it's a deliberate, flagged callback. After publishing, add one
block for the piece: its opening move, its devices and jokes, its hero image, any running
bit it touched. Newest at the bottom. (VOICE_GUIDE.md, "The October 2026 reset", rule 9.)

## Retired outright (Oct 20, Jason's feedback) — do not use again this season
- **Opening on a clock time** ("At 9:34 Monday night…", "At 11:22 Monday night…", "At 12:33
  Sunday morning…", "In London it was a minute past three…"). Used in four of the first
  five recaps.
- **Opening a preview on the commissioner** ("First, the commissioner.", "Jason Schmitt is
  3-0…", "Jason Schmitt has finished second, third…"). Used in three of four previews.
  Jason is one manager of twelve; he gets no more airtime than anyone else.
- **"By the clock on the league's win-probability tracker"** and any narration of the
  tracker's readings.
- **Money Shot tiers** in the preview ("FIVE MONEY SHOTS … ONE MONEY SHOT").
- **"Things I think" numbered lists**, "Factoid That May Interest Only Me", "Good for you,
  ___" sincere beat as a fixed slot.
- **"Now, to the rest of the week."** as the hand-off line.
- **"It leads because Yahoo can't separate them"** / any Game of the Week justified by
  projected closeness.
- **"Programming note: this column is a day late."**
