# Drew Magary — 02 Column Architecture

Magary runs three recurring formats. All three are built on **fixed section names that return every week** with fresh content inside them.

## A. Why Your Team Sucks (WYTS) — the roast template
One post per NFL team, every August. Rigid structure readers know cold:

1. **Cold open** — a short, brutal thesis about the team/city/fanbase. Sometimes a fake FAQ; sometimes the "Some people are fans of X. But many, many more people are NOT fans of X." frame.
2. **Your 20XX record** — the number, one-line gut-punch.
3. **Your coach** — a physical description that becomes a character (the deer-stand autocrat; the dad negotiating at a car dealership).
4. **Your quarterback** — same treatment.
5. **What's new that sucks** — offseason moves, dismantled one by one.
6. **What has always sucked** — the franchise's eternal disease. Owner, stadium, history, city.
7. **What might not suck** — one grudging paragraph. Shortest section. Often ends in a retraction.
8. **HEAR IT FROM [TEAM] FANS!** — reader-submitted one-liners about why their own team sucks, printed raw with first names. Crowd-sourced self-hate. *The section the league should steal outright.*

## B. The Jamboroo — the weekly in-season template
Runs Thursday. Fixed sections in fixed order (skeleton from a 2018 Deadspin edition; Defector version nearly identical):

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Intro essay** | 500–1,500 words on one thing on his mind. Could be football, could be raw water, could be his kid's bus stop. Escalates. |
| **The Games** | Every matchup rated **1 to 5 Throwgasms**. Paragraphs for the interesting ones; empty tiers get **"None."** Paragraphs wander — starts on the Bills, ends on Red Zone Channel philosophy. |
| **Pregame Song That Makes Me Want To Run Through A Goddamn Brick Wall** | A real song, embedded, with a sincere paragraph. |
| **Gregg Easterbrook Memorial Haughty Dipshit Of The Week** | A real article by a pompous person, quoted line by line and destroyed. Named for the TMQ columnist he despised. |
| **Rex Ryan's Lock Of The Week** | A pick, delivered as a fake Rex Ryan monologue that derails into feet. Running record tracked. |
| **Fantasy Player Who Deserves To Die A Slow, Painful Death** | His own fantasy grievance. |
| **Fire This Asshole!** | Running list of coaches on the hot seat, with FIRED! / NOT FIRED! / RETIRED! / WTF?! tags updated weekly. |
| **Great Moments In Poop History** | Reader-submitted shitting-yourself story, reprinted in full, one-line button. |
| **Gametime Snack Of The Week** | Reviewed. |
| **Gametime Cheap Beer Of The Week** | Reader-submitted terrible beer, photo, reviewed. |
| **Jim Tomsula's Lifehack Of The Week!** | Fake Tomsula monologue about beans or similar. |
| **Sunday Afternoon Movie Of The Week For Browns Fans** | One-paragraph movie review for people whose team is unwatchable. |
| **Gratuitous Simpsons Quote** | The closer. Every week. No commentary. |
| **Enjoy the games, everyone.** | Sign-off. |

## C. The Funbag — mailbag
Reader questions (increasingly about anything but sports: butter, crabs, hot dogs, marriage), answered at length with tangents. The **"PROGRAMMING NOTE"** at the top is house style — he tells you what's coming and why the column is short/late.

## D. The line-by-line evisceration (a sub-format)
Used in the Easterbrook section and anywhere he's mad at a text:
```
[Quoted sentence from target]
One-line reaction.
[Next quoted sentence]
Shorter reaction.
[Next]
Two words.
[Next]
Because it is.
```
Reactions get *shorter and more contemptuous* as the quote goes on. The rhythm is the joke.
