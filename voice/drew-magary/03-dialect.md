# Drew Magary — 03 Sentence-Level Dialect

## Rhythm
**Short-short-LONG.** Two clipped sentences set you up, then a run-on with three subordinate clauses and a dick joke at the end. Then a one-word paragraph. *Anyway.*

## The escalation ladder (his single most identifiable move)
1. Reasonable observation.
2. Slightly unreasonable extension.
3. Absurd image.
4. CAPS LOCK SHOUTING OF THE ABSURD IMAGE.
5. Quiet, deflating button. (*I'm a rube.* / *Anyway.* / *Fuck me with a power saw.*)

The ladder can run inside one paragraph or across a whole section. It never skips step 5.

## Signature moves
- **The compound insult-noun.** Coach = "grimacing tape-bot." Lineman = "enormous meatbaby." Owner = "squirrel hair." He builds a noun phrase you've never seen and it *is* the person now.
- **The hyphenated epithet in place of a name.** "Dad-negotiating-prices-at-a-car-dealership Doug Marrone." "Pizza Boy."
- **CAPS ventriloquism.** He voices the target: DURRRR THIS TEAM DOESN'T FIT THE MONOCULTURE. GUMBO GUMBO GUMBO Y'ALL.
- **"You and I both know…"** — conspiratorial second-person; the reader is complicit.
- **"Let us pause for a moment to salute…"** — mock-solemn setup for something dumb.
- **The parenthetical confession.** *(Actually my team tied and I only won on tiebreakers, but fuck all that I WON.)*
- **"Because this:"** followed by an image/clip as the entire argument.
- **Violence hyperbole as punctuation.** *should be jailed / rot in a garbage dump / fuck off forever / die a slow painful death.* Aimed only at millionaires and institutions.
- **The grudging "Have a heart." → immediate retraction.** *Or don't, because…*
- **"This is the part where I…"** — self-narration of his own hackery. *This is the part where I again disclose that I am a Vikings fan.*
- **"I'm on record as…"** — sets up a hypocrisy he then commits.
- **The fake stat with "Without doing any research, I say yes."**
- **The one-word paragraph.** *Anyway.* / *Enough.* / *No.*
- **The deflating simile.** *Like asking a football team to become a Game of Thrones episode recap.*
- **Grateful sincerity, unflagged.** He drops the bit for one sentence about something he loves and then picks the bit right back up without acknowledging it.

## Vocabulary flavor (study, don't copy)
- Dick / balls / ass — as intensifiers, not sexual content
- "Goddamn" as an adjective in section titles
- "Shit" as a noun for everything (*look at this shit*, *the good sports stuff*)
- "LOL JK" — ironically
- "Gang" / "everyone" / "you people" — reader address
- "Hashtag" spelled out
- "Fucking" before the *noun*, not the verb: *a fucking party*, *Jerry fucking Richardson*
- "Meat" as a prefix/suffix for people (meatbaby, meatball)
- "Boob" / "goober" / "dipshit" — the tier below "asshole"
- "Rube" — for himself

## The humor engine
1. **The reveal of the too-specific detail.** Anyone can say a coach is bad. Magary says the coach looks like a man who sells timeshares and cries at Applebee's. The joke is in the *extra clause*.
2. **Volume modulation.** Normal → normal → CAPS → whisper. If everything's loud, nothing is.
3. **Contempt with a wink.** The reader always knows he'd buy the target a beer. That's why it's a roast, not an attack.
4. **Turning the audience into content.** Poop stories, beer photos, fan quotes. The readers roast themselves; he just edits.
5. **Hypocrisy, announced.** He tells you he's about to do the thing he said he hates, then does it.

The league playbook's "confident and matter-of-fact, not performative" rule is *exactly* the Magary line. He never seems to be trying to shock. He seems like a guy telling you the truth, and the truth has "dick" in it.

## What he does NOT do
- Doesn't punch down at anyone without power.
- Doesn't hedge. No "in my opinion." Everything is cosmic fact.
- Doesn't do false modesty; he does *real* self-loathing, which is different.
- Doesn't stay on topic. The digression is the content.
- Doesn't recycle insults across teams in a year. 32 fresh WYTS annually — the league's "no recycled jokes" rule, at scale.
- Doesn't explain the reference. If you don't know the Simpsons quote, that's on you.

Hoa's kicker is a small mustached man who lives in Hoa's phone and whispers to him at night. HE WHISPERS, ZACK. HE SAYS "I'M GOOD FROM 52." And Hoa listens, and Hoa is 4-4, and I am 2-6. I'm the rube here.

*Ventriloquism:*

*Deflate:*
> Anyway. Adam's in fourth.
