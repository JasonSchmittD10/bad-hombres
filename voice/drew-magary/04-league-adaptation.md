# Drew Magary — 04 League Adaptation

## Role in the toolkit
Magary is the **roast engine** — the primary voice for Little Bitch of the Week, matchup previews, member profiles, and the punishment tracker. The Week 9 "Autopsy" wrap-up already lives in this neighborhood. To get closer: more specificity per insult, tighter control of when CAPS fire, and the commissioner losing on the page before anyone else does.

## Sections to port

| Magary section | League version |
|---|---|
| **WYTS** | **"Why Your Team Sucks: [Member]"** — preseason or bye-week feature. One member per post with the full skeleton: Your record / Your draft / What's new that sucks / What has always sucked / What might not suck / **HEAR IT FROM THE LEAGUE** (solicit one-liners from the other 11 guys about the target; print raw with first names) |
| **Throwgasms** | Matchup tiers under a league-specific, offensive unit name. Empty tiers get **"None."** Forces editorial choice — not every game gets a paragraph |
| **Fire This Asshole!** | **"Punishment Watch"** — running tracker of who's closest to last place with tags: SAFE! / COOKED! / IN DENIAL! / PRAYING! Dylan gets a permanent **OWES US A PUNISHMENT!** line |
| **Fantasy Player Who Deserves To Die…** | **"Player Who Ruined Someone's Week"** — the real NFL player who tanked a member, with the member named |
| **Gametime Cheap Beer** | Member-submitted photo of what they were drinking during the loss, reviewed |
| **Rex Ryan's Lock** | **"Pastor Wes's Lock of the Week"** — fake monologue in Wes's voice that starts as a sermon and ends somewhere it shouldn't. Track his fake record. |
| **Jim Tomsula's Lifehack** | A second recurring member voice — David's campaign statements, or Drew's travel itinerary |
| **Great Moments in Poop History** | The league already has the appetite. Solicit stories. Print them whole. |
| **Easterbrook line-by-line** | The group-chat evisceration: quote a member's trade-offer justification sentence by sentence, react to each, reactions get shorter |
| **Gratuitous Simpsons Quote** | Any fixed pop-culture closer the league agrees on. The point is it's *fixed* and *uncommented* |

## The tonal fit
The league's guys are okay being pushed. Magary's rule keeps that healthy: **the target is always a peer** (a fellow manager, a millionaire NFL owner, a bad decision), and **the narrator is always also a loser**. Rage at Chris's trade offers is funny because the commissioner just went 79-point Week 1.

## Notes on member fit
- **Dylan** is the eternal Punishment Watch line — never let it drop.
- **Chris's trade offers** are the natural line-by-line evisceration target.
- **Wes** is the Rex Ryan slot: warm, recurring, derails.
- **David** is the Tomsula slot if you want a second voice: campaign statements, increasingly deranged.
- **Drew's travel** is a running bit: he's at a game somewhere, always.
