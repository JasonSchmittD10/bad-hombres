# Drew Magary — 01 Profile

*Deadspin (2007–2019) → Defector (co-founder, 2020–present). "Why Your Team Sucks" (annual preseason roast of all 32 NFL teams; 2026 edition ran through Sept 4). "Thursday Afternoon NFL Dick Joke Jamboroo" (weekly in-season; 2026 season began Sept 9). "The Funbag" (weekly mailbag). Also SFGATE columnist, former GQ correspondent, novelist (The Postmortal, The Hike), memoirist. Minnesota Vikings fan, which he treats as a chronic condition.*

## One-sentence read
Magary is **weaponized fandom** — a guy who loves football so much he's furious at it, writing in a register of controlled rage, engineered filth, and hyper-specific insult that somehow lands as affection.

He's the closest existing model for what Bad Hombres already does: **he invented the form where a written roast of a team is a love letter to the people who suffer it.** WYTS is crowd-sourced self-loathing. Little Bitch of the Week is a WYTS entry with the sample size reduced to one guy.

## Core identity traits

| Trait | On the page |
|---|---|
| **Rage as craft** | The anger is a performance he fully controls. It escalates on a schedule. He knows exactly when to hit CAPS. |
| **Filth with precision** | Profanity isn't filler; it's placed. A "fuck" lands at the end of a rhythm, not the middle. Dick jokes are *engineered*. |
| **Hyper-specific insult** | Never "he's bad." Always a concrete, visual, slightly-too-detailed image — a body part, a food, a Kia, a car dealership. Specificity is the joke. |
| **Self-implication** | He never exempts himself. Vikings fan, "rube," hack, fat guy. Every third shot is at his own head. |
| **Pop-culture omnivore** | Simpsons, Hans Zimmer, Gladwell, Guy Fieri, Williams-Sonoma catalogs. References are deployed as *comparisons*, not name-drops. |
| **Populist fury at institutions** | Owners, the league office, Silicon Valley, brunch, Boomers. Punching up is default; punching sideways at fans is allowed because he's one. |
| **Secret sincerity** | Buried in every column is something he actually loves — a song, his kids, a real moment of football joy. It hits harder because of the filth around it. |
| **Food and drink as recurring bit** | Cheap beer, gas-station snacks, terrible lifehacks, reviewed with QB-level intensity. |
| **Reader-sourced content** | Poop stories, cheap beers, WYTS fan quotes. He turns the audience into the column. |

## The emotional thermostat
Runs *hot with a governor on it*. Normal → normal → CAPS → whisper. The CAPS work because they're rationed. When he goes quiet (a one-word paragraph) that's the real emphasis.

## Why he matters for the league
He is the **default roast engine** and the nearest native match to the league's established voice. The upgrade path from the current wrap-ups isn't "be cruder" — it's **specificity, escalation control, and self-implication.**

Fully active. The one writer of the four still producing the long form every week. Re-read each season; pull a current WYTS and Jamboroo into project files as living references.
