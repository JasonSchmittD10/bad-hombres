# Drew Magary — 05 Prompt Kit

## The rules (how to fake it)
1. **Open cold** with a thesis sentence that's already an insult. No throat-clearing.
2. **Build every insult as a noun phrase with a visual.** Person → object or creature → one more adjective than necessary.
3. **Use the escalation ladder.** Two normal sentences, one absurd, one CAPS, one deflate.
4. **Implicate yourself** at least once per section.
5. **Ventriloquize the target in CAPS** at least once.
6. **One line-by-line evisceration** of something someone actually said (group chat is the source).
7. **Rate things on a fake scale** and leave a tier empty with "None."
8. **Close a section on a one-word or three-word paragraph.**
9. **Bury one sincere thing** under the filth. Don't flag it.
10. **Fake monologue from a recurring character.** Pick a member; keep the voice consistent week to week; track a fake record.
11. **End on a fixed quote or button**, never a summary.
12. **Profanity goes before nouns and at the ends of rhythms.** Never as a comma.

## Paste-ready prompt
```
Write this week's Bad Hombres [matchup preview / Little Bitch of the Week / member profile] in the Drew Magary register (Why Your Team Sucks / Jamboroo).

Narrator rules: confident, matter-of-fact, filthy, never performative. Every insult is a specific visual noun phrase, not "he sucks." Use the escalation ladder — two normal sentences, one absurd image, one CAPS line, one quiet deflating button — and don't skip the deflate. Ventriloquize at least one member in CAPS. The narrator (commissioner) loses on the page at least once before roasting anyone. One sincere sentence buried somewhere, unflagged. Profanity placed at the ends of rhythms and before nouns — never as filler.

Structure:
- Cold-open thesis (one paragraph, already an insult)
- [Section name]: matchups rated 1–5 on a scale called [league unit]; empty tiers say "None."
- PUNISHMENT WATCH: every team at risk, one line each, ALL-CAPS TAG (SAFE! / COOKED! / OWES US A PUNISHMENT!)
- PASTOR WES'S LOCK OF THE WEEK: fake Wes monologue, starts as sermon, derails, ends with a pick; then "Wes record: X-X"
- One line-by-line evisceration of a group-chat message I paste below
- Fixed closer: [league's chosen quote/format]
- "Enjoy the games, everyone."

Fresh jokes only — nothing from prior weeks. Targets are peers and bad decisions, never anything that could actually hurt someone.

Data: [paste scores, projections, group chat]
```

## QA checklist
- [ ] First sentence is an insult with a visual
- [ ] Every "he's bad" has been replaced with a specific image
- [ ] CAPS appears at most once per section
- [ ] Every CAPS line is followed by a quiet deflate
- [ ] Commissioner takes a shot at himself before anyone else
- [ ] At least one member ventriloquized
- [ ] Empty tiers say "None."
- [ ] One sincere line, unflagged
- [ ] Profanity is placed, not sprinkled
- [ ] Recurring character monologue is in the same voice as last week
- [ ] Closer is the fixed one
- [ ] Nothing recycled

## Reference reads (self-study, not reproduction)
- Any WYTS at defector.com — read the **Vikings** entry for self-implication in its purest form, then a team he hates (Patriots, Cowboys, Steelers) for the ceiling
- A regular-season **Jamboroo** (Deadspin 2012–2019 or Defector 2020–present) — the full skeleton in one place
- **The Funbag** — any week — tangent-as-content
- His **SFGATE** columns — same voice on non-sports targets (Bay Area, parenting, Boomers)
- *Someone Could Get Hurt* (memoir) — the sincere register with the filth turned down, to see what's underneath
