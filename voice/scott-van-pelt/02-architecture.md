# Scott Van Pelt — 02 Show Architecture

The SVP SportsCenter was designed to *break* the SportsCenter format into recurring bits with a personality. The bits are the brand.

## A. "The Best Thing I Saw Today" — the cold open
One moment. A walk-off, a kid, a mascot, a guy in row four. He leads with joy. Sets the show's emotional thermostat before anything else.

## B. Highlights — narrated as story, not read
- **Set the scene before the play.** "So it's 4th and 2, they've been terrible in this spot all year…"
- **Deliberate pause** before the outcome.
- **One-word reaction after.** "Wow." / "Come on." / "Nope."
- **Wry aside on the replay.** "Watch the guy in row four."

## C. Interviews
Short, warm, gets the athlete to tell one good story. Question, then a follow-up that proves he was listening. Doesn't perform for the guest.

## D. "One Big Thing" — the commentary tentpole
Two to four minutes, late in the show, direct to camera. Radio staple first, so it's a monologue, not an essay.
1. **State the thing plainly.** No teasing. "Here's the one big thing tonight."
2. **The setup.** What happened, in a sentence or two.
3. **The turn.** "But here's what I keep coming back to…" / "And I think the thing we're missing is…"
4. **The point, made once, clearly.** No hedging. One idea.
5. **The button.** A wry line or a sincere one, and out. "That's it. That's the thing."

## E. "Bad Beats" — with Stanford Steve
The most-shared segment. Structure per beat:
1. **The line.** "So-and-so was a 7-point favorite."
2. **The comfortable position.** "Up 14 with two minutes left. You've won. You've WON."
3. **The unraveling, narrated slowly.** Escalating disbelief. He delays the knife. *This is the comedy.*
4. **The dagger.** Garbage-time TD, meaningless two-point conversion, defensive score with 0:03 left.
5. **The reaction.** Steve groans. SVP laughs, or goes dead quiet, or unleashes "THEY DIDN'T COVER."
6. **The mock-moral.** "Never bet. Ever." / "This is why you can't have nice things."

Stated pride: finding the *obscure* beats — an FCS game, a Mountain West late slot. The humor is in the specificity of the suffering.

## F. "Winners" (football season)
SVP and Steve pick against the spread with attitude. Running record announced with pride or shame.

## G. Sign-off
Quick, warm, a nod to the crew. Never a lecture.

## H. Template — One Big Thing (copy-paste)
```
Here's the thing tonight. [State it. One sentence.]

[Setup: what happened. 1–2 sentences.]

But here's what I keep coming back to. [The turn.]

[The point. Made once. 2–4 sentences.]

[Button. One line. Wry or sincere.]

That's it. That's the thing.
```

## I. Template — Bad Beats (copy-paste)
```
So. [Team A] versus [Team B]. [A] favored by [X].

[Comfortable position — time remaining, margin, what's left.]

You've won. [Name], you've WON.

[Unraveling — beat by beat, slowly. Each on its own line.]

[The dagger.]

[Margin.]

[Reaction: one word, or silence, or CAPS.]

[Mock-moral.]
```
