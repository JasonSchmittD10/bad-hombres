# Blending Guide — Four Voices, One League
*How the King / Magary / Berry / Van Pelt dialects map onto Bad Hombres content, when to use which, and how to mix them without producing mush.*

## 3. Blending rules

**Rule 1 — One primary voice per post.** A wrap-up is *Berry-structured*. A preview is *Magary-tiered*. A One Big Thing is *SVP*. Seasoning is a device, not a second voice. If a reader can't tell whose skeleton it is, it has no skeleton.

**Rule 2 — Borrow devices, not vocabulary.** Take Magary's escalation ladder; don't take his words. Take Berry's "Get this:" beat; write your own stat. Take King's section names (format, fair game); write your own Factoids. Take SVP's delay; write your own dagger.

**Rule 3 — Profanity is a button, not a base.** Three of four models don't curse; the league does. Crude content lives in the *nouns* (award names, team names, the specific comparison) and in the *button* (the last line of a paragraph). It does not live in the *intensifiers*. "Fucking" before every noun is noise. One "Fuck you" as Zack's quoted text is a punchline. (The playbook's "confident and matter-of-fact, not performative" rule, restated mechanically.)

**Rule 4 — Every post needs one sincere beat.** King: "Good for you." Magary: the buried thing he loves. Berry: the confession. SVP: "serious for a second." Zero sincerity reads as trying too hard. Two is a Hallmark card.

**Rule 5 — Fixed sections are a feature.** All four voices are built on recurring bits with fixed names. The reader's pleasure is in the *return* of the bit. Have 4–6 fixed section names in the same order every week; content inside is fresh. Structure repeats; jokes don't.

**Rule 6 — Self-implication licenses the roast.** The commissioner has to lose on the page at least once per post — a bad start, a checked score, a dumb waiver claim — before he calls anyone a bitch. Go one layer more embarrassing than feels comfortable.

**Rule 7 — "The Rest" is a Berry problem with a Berry fix.** Chain-link it ("Speaking of losing… From Hoa to Wes…") so it reads as momentum. Flat lists read as fatigue.

**Rule 8 — Ventriloquize members, don't just describe them.** Magary's Rex Ryan and Tomsula; SVP's Stanford Steve. Pick one or two members who become *recurring voices* with a consistent register and a tracked fake record. Highest-return bit in the toolkit; the league hasn't fully deployed it.

## 4. Anti-patterns — what "trying too hard" looks like in each voice

| Voice | Failure mode | Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| **King** | Parody without affection | Coffee jokes with no actual coffee content; "I think" items that are sarcastic instead of sincere. King works when the narrator *means it*. |
| **Magary** | Volume without specificity | CAPS in every paragraph; "fucking" as a comma; insults that are just "he sucks." The Magary tell is the *extra clause*, not the swear. |
| **Berry** | Intro without confession | A "personal story" that's just a setup with no vulnerability. Readers smell a fake open. Also: rushing to the lists. |
| **SVP** | Explaining the joke | A sentence after "That's a choice." Narrating the dagger before it lands. The pause *is* the content. |
| **All** | Recycled bits | Same insult for the same guy two weeks running. Magary writes 32 fresh WYTS a year; the league can do 12. |

## 5. Suggested fixed weekly skeleton (v2, voices assigned)

A proposal — the current playbook skeleton works. Same shape, each slot given to its strongest voice.

1. **[Berry] The Open** — scene → confession → disclaimer → turn → bridge → "Let's get to it."
2. **[Berry] Housekeeping with a wink** — dues, punishment, reminders, acknowledged as such.
3. **[SVP] The Best Thing I Saw This Week** — one earned compliment. Short.
4. **[Magary] Game of the Week** — full escalation ladder, one CAPS moment, deflate.
5. **[Magary/Berry] Featured Matchups** — Magary specificity, Berry stat framing.
6. **[Berry] The Rest** — chain-linked, "Speaking of…"
7. **[SVP] Bad Beats** — the week's closest/cruelest loss, delay → dagger.
8. **[Magary] Little Bitch of the Week** — one-man WYTS.
9. **[King] Hardware** — award names as King section headers; one Factoid That May Interest Only Me.
10. **[Magary] Punishment Watch** — Fire This Asshole!-style tracker with tags.
11. **[Magary] Next Week** — tiered previews; empty tiers say "None."
12. **[King] Ten Things I Think I Think** — optional, big weeks; item 10 goes personal.
13. **[Magary/King] Closer** — a fixed pop-culture quote or a haiku. Pick one; never change it.

## 6. Prompting the repos
The highest-leverage instruction: **"Use the [writer] skeleton from `02-architecture.md`, section names included, and the devices from `03-dialect.md`. Do not use his vocabulary. Fresh jokes only. One sincere beat. League register for the content, [writer] register for the narrator."**

That last clause is the whole trick. The narrator is the borrowed voice. The league is the content. The gap between them is where the comedy lives.

Each `05-prompt-kit.md` has a paste-ready version of this for its writer.
