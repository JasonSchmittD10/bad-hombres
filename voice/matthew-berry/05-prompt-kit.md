# Matthew Berry — 05 Prompt Kit

## The rules (how to fake it)
1. **Open on a scene, not a summary.** A specific moment, a year, a line of dialogue.
2. **Confess something slightly embarrassing** in the first 300 words. Not a joke — a real feeling. The embarrassing *specific*.
3. **Disclaim the confession.** "I know that's crazy."
4. **Bridge to fantasy in one sentence.** Then "Let's get to it."
5. **Do housekeeping with a wink.** Acknowledge the reminder as a reminder.
6. **Every entry: hook → "Get this:" → 3–6 stats with comps → "so" sentence → callback button.**
7. **Chain-link the minor entries** with "Speaking of…" / "From X to Y…"
8. **One CAPS moment per section, max.** Period. Separated. Words. for beats.
9. **Stage a reader objection and answer it.**
10. **Callback the intro theme in the closer.** Last line rhymes with the first scene.
11. **Thank someone.** Genuinely.
12. **No asterisked profanity.** Either say the word as a button or don't say it.

## Paste-ready prompt
```
Write this week's Bad Hombres wrap-up open and list sections in the Matthew Berry / Love/Hate register.

Intro (400–700 words): open on a specific scene from my life (I'll give you the raw material below), widen to why it mattered, then CONFESS the embarrassing specific — not "I lost" but the thing I actually did (checked a score too many times, cared about the wrong thing). Disclaim it ("I realize that's insane"). Turn: connect it to this week's league storyline. One-sentence bridge. Then "Let's get to it."

Housekeeping: one short paragraph of reminders (dues/punishment/payout), acknowledged as reminders, with a wink.

Featured matchups: each entry = hook (joke or staged reader objection) → "Get this:" → 3–6 stats with league-history comps → "So, to recap:" plain-English → callback to the hook, escalated.

The Rest: one chain-linked paragraph using "Speaking of…" and "From X to Y…" transitions. Every team gets one sentence. Must read as momentum, not fatigue.

Closer: last line calls back the opening scene. Thank one person.

Register: league content is crude (team names, award names, the comparisons); profanity is allowed only as a paragraph's final button, never as an intensifier, never asterisked. One CAPS moment per section max. Fresh jokes only.

Raw material for the intro: [personal anecdote]
Data: [scores, standings, group chat]
```

## QA checklist
- [ ] First sentence is a scene, not a thesis
- [ ] Confession is specific and a little embarrassing
- [ ] Confession is disclaimed
- [ ] One-sentence bridge → "Let's get to it."
- [ ] Housekeeping acknowledges itself
- [ ] Every entry has "Get this:" and a callback button
- [ ] "The Rest" is chain-linked, not bulleted
- [ ] At most one CAPS beat per section
- [ ] Profanity only at the ends of paragraphs
- [ ] No asterisks
- [ ] Closer rhymes with the open
- [ ] Someone gets thanked
- [ ] Nothing recycled

## Reference reads (self-study, not reproduction)
- **Final Love/Hate (Aug 2025, NBC/Rotoworld)** — in project context. The Perry intro is the platonic confession-and-turn.
- **"Love, Hate and letting go" (ESPN, Aug 2018)** — the column's origin story: Rotoworld editors defending the "weird mix of life stories, fantasy sports and pop culture jokes." The *mix* was always the product.
- **Any "10 Lists of 10"** — the list-form voice, tighter and denser
- **"10 Facts" weekly columns (Sept 2025–)** — the current stripped form; how the joke-per-fact cadence survives without the intro
- **Fantasy Football Happy Hour** (YouTube/Peacock) — the spoken cadence, nearly identical to the written one
- *Fantasy Life* (book, 2013) — the confessional register at chapter length
