# Peter King — 05 Prompt Kit

## The rules (how to fake it)
1. **Open on a specific moment** — time, place, person. Narrate it like you were in the room.
2. **Quote people.** If nobody said anything, describe what they texted you and when.
3. **Every stat gets a historical comp.** "Only three teams since 2019 have…"
4. **Use the full skeleton** from `02-architecture.md` with the real section names. Readers know them.
5. **Ten Things: exactly ten, every item starts "I think," item 10 gets sub-lettered** into the personal stuff.
6. **One coffee note, one beer note, one travel gripe.** Name the brand. Be sincere.
7. **End on a haiku.** 5-7-5. Play it straight.
8. **Praise someone by name** with "Good for you."
9. **Zero profanity.** The crude content lives in the facts, not the voice.
10. **Never be short.** If it feels done, add a Factoid.

## Paste-ready prompt
```
Write this week's Bad Hombres wrap-up in the Peter King / Football Morning in America register.

Narrator rules: earnest, reportorial, zero profanity, zero sarcasm. The narrator does not know the team names and award names are crude — he reports them the way he'd report a Brady stat line. Every stat gets a comp ("only X times in league history…"). Quote members by full name with a timestamp and location for where they were when it happened (invent plausibly, mark as "I'm told").

Structure, in this order, with these exact section names:
- Lead (time/place/person cold open on the biggest story; one bold subhead pivot to a second story)
- Offensive Player of the Week / Coach of the Week / Goat of the Week
- Quotes of the Week (Roman numerals, italic context line under each — source from the group chat I paste below)
- Stat of the Week
- Factoid That May Interest Only Me
- Coffeenerdness / Beernerdness (what a member was drinking when he made a decision)
- Ten Things I Think I Think (exactly 10; every item starts "I think"; item 10 = non-league thoughts, sub-lettered a–f, include one "Good for you, [name]")
- The Adieu Haiku (5-7-5, sincere)

Fresh jokes only — nothing recycled from prior weeks. One sincere beat somewhere. Length: long. If it feels done, add a Factoid.

Data: [paste scores, standings, group chat]
```

## QA checklist (run before posting)
- [ ] Opens with a clock time and a place
- [ ] At least three direct quotes with attribution lines
- [ ] Every number has a comp
- [ ] "Goat" is sympathetic, not gleeful
- [ ] Ten Things has exactly ten items and each starts "I think"
- [ ] Item 10 goes non-league and is sub-lettered
- [ ] Contains "Good for you, ___"
- [ ] No profanity in the narrator's voice
- [ ] No sarcasm in the narrator's voice
- [ ] Ends on a 5-7-5 haiku
- [ ] Nothing recycled from last week

## Reference reads (self-study, not reproduction)
- Any FMIA column, 2018–2024 (profootballtalk.nbcsports.com/category/fmia) — pick a regular-season Monday to see the full skeleton
- The Feb 26, 2024 retirement column ("It's Time. Who's Complaining? Not Me") — the most self-aware he ever got about the format and why he wouldn't shorten it
- Defector, "10 Things I Think I Think On Peter King's Retirement" (Feb 2024) — a parodist's affectionate breakdown of what the format costs to do
- Old Kissing Suzy Kolber / Deadspin King-parody posts — the negative space; what people mocked tells you the tells
- *Monday Morning Quarterback: A Fully Caffeinated Guide* (book, 2009) — the format frozen in print
