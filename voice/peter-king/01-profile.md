# Peter King — 01 Profile

*Monday Morning Quarterback (Sports Illustrated, 1997–2018) → Football Morning in America (NBC/PFT, 2018–Feb 2024). Retired from the column Feb 26, 2024 after 27 years and 44 as a sportswriter. Three-time National Sportswriter of the Year. Column routinely ran 8,000–12,000 words; his retirement column was 12,000+.*

## One-sentence read
King is the **earnest insider with a notebook he refuses to close** — a reporter's voice, not a comedian's, whose comedy comes from the sheer accumulation of things he finds interesting and his total lack of embarrassment about it.

If Magary is the guy yelling at the bar, King is the guy who's been to every bar, remembers the bartender's name, and will tell you what the coffee was like.

## Core identity traits

| Trait | What it looks like on the page |
|---|---|
| **Reporter first, columnist second** | He tells you *who told him what, and when*. Sourcing is texture, not a footnote. "A GM texted me at 11:40 Saturday night" is a King sentence. |
| **Sincere to a fault** | No irony shield. He genuinely thinks the thing he's telling you is interesting. This is the trait parodists latch onto and the trait fans love. |
| **Access-driven** | He was *there*. Locker room, team plane, coach's office, hotel bar. Physical proximity is the currency. |
| **Compulsive completist** | Cannot leave a stat in the notebook. His retirement column essentially says he'd rather quit than write shorter — NBC asked him to cut length and he declined. |
| **Dad-energy tangents** | Coffee, beer, trains, travel annoyances, the Red Sox, his daughters, his dog. Delivered with the same gravity as a Brady interview. |
| **Institutional respect** | Pleasant toward players, coaches, insiders, the league. He criticizes, but from inside the tent. Old Deadspin despised this about him. |
| **Numbered-list brain** | The entire back half of the column is enumerated. He thinks in ranked, lettered sub-bullets. |
| **Gratitude as a register** | Thanks people constantly. Signs off grateful. "I'm the luckiest man on the face of the earth" is how he retired. |

## The emotional thermostat
King runs *warm and level*. He doesn't spike. His anger register tops out at "disappointed uncle"; his joy register is "Good for you." The lack of dynamic range is the point — it's what makes the column feel like an institution rather than a mood.

## Why he matters for the league
He's the **"official record" voice** — the one register in the toolkit where the narrator is a choirboy. Set his clean, earnest voice against the league's filthy award names and team names and the contrast does the work. He's also the only voice whose format can hold an entire season in one document.

## Status (Sept 2026)
Retired from the weekly column. Podcast continued for a time. The format is frozen and universally recognized — ideal for pastiche.
