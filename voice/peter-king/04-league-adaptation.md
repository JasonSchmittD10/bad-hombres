# Peter King — 04 League Adaptation

## Role in the toolkit
King is the **"official record" voice**. Reach for him when:
- The wrap-up should feel like an institution — the Monday morning document everyone reads on the toilet
- You're writing a **season-in-review, midseason omnibus, or playoff preview** — only his cadence holds a whole season without collapsing
- You want the **satirically formal/bureaucratic register** the league already uses for governance memos. King's earnestness is the natural bridge: a commissioner who writes "Good for you, Hoa" with a straight face about a kicker is funny *because* it's King-sincere about something stupid.

## The comedic play
King's voice is clean. So the **crude league content does the offending while the narrator stays a choirboy.** The team names, the award names, the scores, the punishments — those are the facts. King reports them the way he'd report a Brady stat line. The gap is the joke.

**Do not** make King curse. **Do not** make King sarcastic. If he sounds like he's in on the joke, the joke dies.

## Sections to port directly

| King section | League version |
|---|---|
| Offensive Player of the Week | Highest individual player score across all rosters, with the roster owner named |
| Defensive Player of the Week | Highest D/ST — dovetails with "The Iron Curtain" award |
| Coach of the Week | The manager whose waiver/lineup decision won a game |
| **Goat of the Week** | The manager whose bench outscored his starters, or who benched the week's top scorer. Delivered with *sympathy* |
| **Quotes of the Week** | Lifted from the group chat, numbered I/II/III, each with a deadpan italic context line |
| Tweets of the Week | Group-chat screenshots, reproduced, one-line reaction |
| Stat of the Week | The week's defining number with a league-history comp |
| **Factoid That May Interest Only Me** | The obscure member-specific stat: Adam's four-year points-against total, Dylan's outstanding punishment, Hoa's kicker's share of his team points |
| King of the Road | Whoever set a lineup from an airport / a wedding / a hospital waiting room |
| Coffeenerdness / Beernerdness | What a member was drinking when he made the decision |
| **Ten Things I Think I Think** | The commissioner's ten verdicts; item 10 goes to non-league life (babies, moves, the shed, someone's new grill) |
| **The Adieu Haiku** | Weekly closer. Play it straight |

## Sample 1 — Lead + Factoid + Ten Things (Week 9, 2025 data as placeholder)

> It was 4:17 p.m. Eastern on Sunday when David Hurst, general manager and principal owner of Drain the Swamp, looked at his phone in what I'm told was a Costco parking lot and saw the number: 164.24. He did not, according to a source with knowledge of the situation, say anything. He put the phone back in his pocket and went inside for paper towels.
>
> That's the thing about the week's biggest performance in this league. It arrived quietly, from a team that had been 2-4 three weeks earlier, and it landed on a Rags-to-Richardson roster that scored 76.44. Eighty-seven point eight points of separation. I looked it up: only twice in the last three seasons has a margin in this league exceeded 85 points, and Dylan Greene has been on the wrong end of both.
>
> Good for you, David. Really.
>
> **Factoid That May Interest Only Me:** Suck My Diggs has scored fewer points than its opponent in 61 percent of its games since 2022 and has finished last zero times. Adam Schmitt is the Ripken of avoiding consequences.
>
> **Ten Things I Think I Think**
> 1. I think the Lenovo Civil War is the matchup of the week and it's not close, and I think the loser has to change his team name.
> 2. I think King In The North's 76.92 projection is the lowest I have seen in this column, and I think Erick will beat it, which is not the same as saying he'll win.
> 3. I think four teams at 2-6 in Week 9 is not parity. It's something else, and I don't have a word for it.
> …
> 10. I think these are my non-football thoughts of the week:
>    a. Pastor Wes, congratulations on the new grill. I want a full report.
>    b. Coffeenerdness: Whatever Hoa was drinking when he started his kicker over a functioning wide receiver — I'd like some. Or, I would not.
>    c. Beernerdness: Zack, please stop sending me pictures of Busch Light. It is not a craft beer. It is a decision.
>    d. Good for you, Matt, for setting a lineup from a time zone where the games start at 10 a.m. Not everyone would.
>
> **The Adieu Haiku**
> *Four teams at two-six.*
> *No one is safe from last place.*
> *Dylan, do your chores.*

## Sample 2 — Hardware block (Goat / Quotes / Stat)

> **Goat of the Week**
> Erick DeLeon, King In The North. Left 31.4 points on his bench, including a tight end who scored more than his entire starting receiving corps. He lost by 13. I say this with affection: Erick, the lineup lock is at 1 p.m. It has always been at 1 p.m.
>
> **Quotes of the Week**
> I. "I'm not mad. I'm just going to bed."
> *— Zack Moore, Sewer Pigeons, Sunday, 11:52 p.m., after his fifth loss in six weeks, in a message that was followed by four more messages.*
>
> II. "Who is Emari Demercado."
> *— Chris Dambro, Judge Jeudy, without a question mark, Sunday afternoon, after starting Emari Demercado.*
>
> III. "The Lord works in mysterious ways."
> *— Wes Jones, MikeLee4 Prez, on being asked how he won.*
>
> **Stat of the Week**
> 1,141.68. That's the combined points-against for Sewer Pigeons through eight weeks, the most in the league by 74. Zack has not played a bad team all year. I checked. Nobody has, but Zack least of all.

## Notes on member fit
- **Wes (Pastor)** is the natural "Good for you" target — King's warmth suits the group's dad.
- **David's coup** is best handled King-style as institutional news: *"The Hurst camp declined comment."*
- **Dylan's unfulfilled punishment** is a Factoid, every week, forever.
- **Adam** gets the Ripken treatment — stat-based, never personal.
