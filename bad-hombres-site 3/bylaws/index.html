The Contract

Official By-Laws

The binding contract of the Bad Hombres Fantasy Football League.

§Preamble & Legal Disclaimer›

PREAMBLE. These by-laws constitute a binding contract between all participating members of this fantasy football league. By paying entry fees and participating in league activities, all members agree to be bound by these rules and procedures. This league is a game of skill where outcomes reflect the "relative" knowledge and skill of participants and are determined predominantly by accumulated statistical results of NFL players.

Legal Disclaimer: Participants acknowledge that fantasy football involves elements of chance and skill, and that no guarantee of winning is made or implied. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of America.

ILeague Overview & Formation›

1.1 League Mission

This league exists to provide competitive fantasy football entertainment among friends and acquaintances, with monetary stakes to ensure serious participation and engagement.

1.2 League Authority

These by-laws supersede all platform default settings and serve as the ultimate authority for league governance. Where conflicts exist between platform rules and these by-laws, these by-laws shall govern.

1.3 Season Definition

The league season begins with the draft and concludes with the final championship game and prize distribution.

IIGoverning Body & Authority›

2.1 Commissioner Authority

Jason Schmitt shall serve as League Commissioner with the following powers and responsibilities:

  • Day-to-day league administration
  • Transaction processing and lineup enforcement
  • Initial dispute resolution
  • Platform management and technical support
  • Communication of league announcements

2.2 Commissioner Limitations

The Commissioner may not:

  • Veto trades involving their own team without Executive Committee approval
  • Change scoring settings mid-season
  • Modify by-laws without proper voting procedures
  • Process transactions that provide unfair advantage to any specific team

2.3 Executive Committee

An Executive Committee consisting of three (3) non-commissioner members shall be elected annually to:

  • Review disputed trades and decisions
  • Resolve conflicts involving the Commissioner
  • Approve major rule interpretations
  • Oversee constitutional amendments

2.4 Succession Planning

In the event the Commissioner becomes unavailable, the Executive Committee shall appoint an interim Commissioner to complete the season.

IIIMembership Requirements & Responsibilities›

3.1 Membership Qualifications

All members must:

  • Pay entry fees by established deadline
  • Maintain active participation throughout the season
  • Comply with all league rules and conduct standards

3.2 Member Responsibilities

Each member shall:

  • Set valid starting lineups weekly
  • Respond to trade proposals within 48 hours
  • Participate in draft proceedings
  • Maintain disrespectful communication
  • Pay all fees and penalties on time

3.3 Removal Procedures

Members may be removed by two-thirds (2/3) vote of all other members for:

  • Non-payment of fees
  • Consistent failure to set lineups (3+ weeks)
  • Violation of last place punishment fulfillment policy
  • Violation of platform terms of service
IVLeague Structure & Organization›

4.1 League Composition

The league consists of exactly twelve (12) teams competing in head-to-head format.

4.2 Regular Season

The regular season shall run from Week 1 through Week 14 of the NFL season, with each team playing fourteen (14) games.

4.3 Schedule Format

  • Head-to-head matchups each week
  • No divisions (single league format)
  • Schedule determined by platform algorithm
  • No repeat opponents until all teams have been played

4.4 Platform Integration

Yahoo Sports serves as the official platform for all league activities, scoring, and recordkeeping.

VScoring System›

5.1 Scoring Overview

The league employs fractional scoring with negative points enabled for certain categories.

5.2 Passing

  • Passing Touchdowns: 4 pts
  • Interceptions: -2 pts
  • Passing Yards: 1 pt per 25 yards
  • Pick Sixes Thrown: -2 pts
  • 40+ Yard Passing TDs: +1 bonus

5.3 Rushing

  • Rushing Touchdowns: 6 pts
  • Rushing Yards: 1 pt per 10 yards
  • 40+ Yard Runs: +2 bonus
  • 40+ Yard Rushing TDs: +2 bonus

5.4 Receiving

  • Receiving Touchdowns: 6 pts
  • Receptions: 1 pt each
  • Receiving Yards: 1 pt per 10 yards
  • 40+ Yard Receptions: +2 bonus
  • 40+ Yard Receiving TDs: +2 bonus

5.5 Kicker

  • Field Goal Total Yards: 1 pt per 10 yards
  • PAT Made: +1 / PAT Missed: -2
  • FG Missed 0-49 yds: -1 (each range)
  • FG Missed 50+ yds: 0

5.6 Defense / Special Teams

  • Points Allowed 0: +15 · 1-6: +10 · 7-13: +7 · 14-20: +4 · 21-27: 0 · 28-34: -1 · 35+: -4
  • Sacks: +1 · INT: +2 · Fumble Rec: +2 · TD: +6 · Safety: +2 · Blocked Kick: +2
  • Kick/Punt Return TD: +6 · 4th Down Stops: +0.5 · Three-and-Outs Forced: +0.5 · Extra Points Returned: +2

5.7 Stat Corrections

Official NFL stat corrections will be applied if announced before 6:00 AM ET on the Thursday following the game week. Corrections announced after this deadline will not be applied retroactively.

VIRoster Management›

6.1 Roster Composition

Each team roster shall consist of:

  • 1 QB · 2 WR · 2 RB · 1 TE
  • 1 W/R/T Flex
  • 1 K · 1 DEF
  • 6 Bench (BN) · 1 Injured Reserve (IR)

6.2 Starting Lineup Requirements

All teams must submit valid starting lineups by the deadline for each position. Failure to do so will result in the platform's automatic selection.

6.3 Anti-Tanking Measures

Teams that fail to set a valid starting lineup will receive a warning. After two violations, the Commissioner may set optimal lineups for that team for the remainder of the season.

VIIDraft Procedures›

7.1 Draft Format

The league shall conduct a snake draft format where the draft order reverses each round.

7.2 Draft Date & Time

Determined by an availability poll conducted by the Commissioner at least three weeks prior to the desired draft window. The option receiving the highest percentage of member availability wins; ties are broken by the Commissioner.

7.3 Draft Order Determination

Determined annually by the Executive Committee using a unique and entertaining method, including but not limited to:

  • Kentucky Derby horse assignments, order set by race results
  • NASCAR finishing positions
  • Random number generator
  • Other creative methods

The method must be announced at least two weeks prior to the draft. All members must participate.

7.4 Draft Pick Trading

Draft pick trading is NOT permitted in this league.

7.5 Draft Conduct

There is none.

VIIISeason Operations›

8.1 Weekly Matchups

Matchups are determined by the Yahoo Sports platform algorithm and cannot be manually adjusted except in cases of technical error.

8.2 Lineup Deadlines

All lineup changes must be made before the scheduled kickoff time of the individual player's game.

8.3 Weekly Awards

The league distributes weekly awards throughout the regular season (Weeks 1–14) at $9 per week, totaling $126. Specific criteria are set by the Executive Committee and announced by the Commissioner prior to each league year, recognizing statistical achievements, performances, or entertaining league moments.

IXTrading Rules & Procedures›

9.1 Trade Approval Process

  • All trades reviewed for a 48-hour period following acceptance
  • Trades vetoed only for clear collusion or a team clearly not attempting to improve
  • A veto requires 50% of non-involved members to vote against
  • Trade reviews conducted by all managers via Yahoo Fantasy

9.2 Trade Deadline

All trades must be completed by 11:59 PM ET on the league trade deadline (late November). No trades processed after this deadline.

9.3 Prohibited Trades

  • Draft pick trading is not permitted
  • Players-for-money trades are prohibited
  • Lopsided trades may be subject to veto
XWaiver Wire & Free Agency›

10.1 Waiver Processing

  • Waiver claims process on Tuesday at game time
  • Dropped players clear waivers for 48 hours before becoming free agents
  • Waiver priority determined by FAB, with reverse order of standings as tiebreak

10.2 Free Agency

Players not claimed on waivers become free agents, available immediately on a first-come, first-served basis.

10.3 Waiver Wire Integrity

No waiver claims may be processed manually by the Commissioner except in cases of verified platform technical errors with proper documentation.

10.4 Technology Failures

If platform issues prevent a team from making claims, the Commissioner may process them if notified before the deadline with proof (screenshots or error messages required).

XIFinancial Management›

11.1 Entry Fees

  • Buy-in: $100 per team
  • Total Prize Pool: $1,200
  • All fees due 2 weeks prior to the draft

11.2 Late Payment Policy

Late payments incur a $5 weekly penalty, added to The Commissioner's Award winnings.

11.3 Prize Distribution

Primary Prizes: 1st Place 60% = $720 · 2nd Place 20% = $200 · 3rd Place 10% = $100.

  • Most Points Scored (Reg. Season): $20
  • You Got Dicked (most points against): $10
  • The Commissioner's Award (sportsmanship or entertainment): $15
  • Most Points in a Single Week: $10
  • You Suck (lowest points): $1
  • 14 Weekly Awards × $9 = $126

11.4 Prize Distribution Timeline

Distribution occurs within two weeks after season conclusion. All entry fees are non-refundable once the season begins.

XIIPlayoffs & Championship›

12.1 Playoff Format

Six (6) teams qualify based on regular season performance. The tournament spans Weeks 15, 16, and 17.

12.2 Seeding & Bracket

  • Top 6 teams by record qualify
  • Seeds 1 & 2 receive first-round byes
  • Week 15: 3v6 and 4v5 (Wildcard)
  • Week 16: Semifinals vs top seeds
  • Week 17: Championship Game

12.3 Playoff Reseeding

Reseeding is enabled. After each round, the lowest remaining seed faces the highest remaining seed.

12.4 Playoff Tiebreakers

In tied playoff matchups, the higher seed automatically advances.

12.5 Eliminated Team Restrictions

Eliminated teams have rosters locked and may not make waiver claims or free agent acquisitions during the playoff period.

12.6 Playoff Roster Management

  • No max limit on player acquisitions per week during playoffs
  • Standard waiver/FA rules apply to remaining teams
  • All other roster rules remain in effect
XIIITiebreaker Procedures›

13.1 Regular Season Tiebreakers

Applied in order:

  • Overall record (wins-losses)
  • Total points scored during regular season
  • Head-to-head record between tied teams
  • Points scored in head-to-head matchups between tied teams

13.2 Playoff Tiebreakers

For playoff matchups ending in ties: higher playoff seed advances.

13.3 Tiebreaker Consistency

Tiebreakers cannot be changed during the season and must be clearly defined before Week 1.

XIVLast Place Punishment›

14.1 Annual Determination

Each off-season, members vote on a punishment for the 12th-place team. It must be approved by a simple majority of all members.

14.2 Punishment Criteria

  • Humiliating but not physically dangerous or illegal
  • Completable within a reasonable timeframe (typically 6 months)
  • No undue financial burden beyond normal league costs
  • Documentable for league entertainment (photos/videos encouraged)

14.3 Punishment Examples

  • Public embarrassment challenges
  • Costume wearing in public places
  • Social media requirements
  • League event hosting duties
  • Creative content creation for league entertainment

14.4 Completion Requirements

The last place team must complete the punishment within the timeframe set by the vote. Failure may result in: first violation — forfeiture of vote on next year's punishment plus mandatory makeup punishment; second violation — expulsion from the league.

14.5 Documentation & Verification

The last place finisher must provide adequate proof of completion as determined by league consensus. The Executive Committee verifies completion and may require additional evidence.

XVConduct Standards & Discipline›

15.1 Acceptable Conduct

  • Competitive trash talk is encouraged and expected
  • Humor and banter are part of league culture
  • Creative team names and roster moves are welcomed

15.2 “Also Acceptable” Conduct

(Yes, the by-laws really say this — read at your own risk.)

  • Personal attacks on family, religion, or sensitive personal matters
  • Hate speech or discriminatory language
  • Threats of violence or harassment
  • Collusion with other teams

15.3 Prohibited Conduct

  • Collusion with other teams

15.4 Disciplinary Actions

  • First offense: Official warning
  • Second offense: Loss of weekly award eligibility
  • Third offense: Suspension from league communications
  • Severe violations: Immediate expulsion with loss of fees
XVIDispute Resolution›

16.1 Initial Resolution

All disputes go first to the Commissioner for review and resolution within 48 hours.

16.2 Executive Committee Review

If the Commissioner is involved or cannot resolve it, the matter goes to the Executive Committee for a final decision within 72 hours.

16.3 League Vote

For major constitutional disputes, a league-wide vote may be called requiring a two-thirds majority.

16.4 Documentation Requirements

All disputes and resolutions must be documented in writing and made available to members.

XVIIRule Changes & Amendments›

17.1 Amendment Process

  • Proposed changes submitted in writing during off-season
  • Two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of all members
  • 72-hour notice period for voting
  • No amendments during active season unless unanimous consent

17.2 Emergency Procedures

For urgent technical or platform issues, the Commissioner may make temporary adjustments with Executive Committee approval, subject to league ratification.

17.3 Version Control

All amendments shall be dated, numbered, and distributed to all members. Previous versions are maintained for historical reference.

XVIIIMiscellaneous Provisions›

18.1 Platform Integration

Yahoo Sports serves as the official scoring and transaction platform. Settings must conform to these by-laws where possible.

18.2 Communication

Official league communications shall be conducted through the Bad Hombres iMessage chat.

18.3 Record Keeping

The Commissioner shall maintain records of all transactions, disputes, and league decisions for historical reference.

18.4 Tax Responsibilities

Winners are responsible for reporting prizes as income per applicable tax laws. The league does not provide tax advice or documentation.
